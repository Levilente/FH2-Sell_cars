# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- New **SELL CAR** entry in the garage action menu (Garage > My Cars > a car), right after *Remove car*. *Remove car* itself is unchanged.
- Choosing it opens a small menu with the price in its title and two entries, **SELL CAR** and **BACK**, selectable with the controller and clickable with the mouse.
- Sale price = 50% of the model's base price + 30% of what was paid for the car's upgrades (both configurable in `SellCar.ini`).
- Update checks through the platform (the title screen tells you about a new release).
